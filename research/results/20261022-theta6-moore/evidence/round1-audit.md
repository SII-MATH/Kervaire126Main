## 1. Theta5: validated finite reduction

Work in the classical 2-primary stable homotopy ring. From AimPaper/main.tex
2234-2239, citing IWX, `pi_62 = (Z/2)^4` with one theta5 direction at Adams
filtration 2 and other directions at filtrations 6, 8, and 10. The set of theta5
choices is the affine coset of the three-dimensional higher-filtration subgroup,
so it has eight members. This uses the classical 2-primary statement, not an
assertion about arbitrary integral or synthetic classes.

For even-degree elements of order two,

    (theta + a)^2 - theta^2 = a^2.

The two cross terms are equal by graded commutativity and sum to zero by
`2 theta = 0`. For differences among the order-two classes the square map is
additive. The same algebraic step is explicitly proved in
`KIP126/Main/Internal/Choice/Filtration.lean:15`, with the order-two premise.

Let `a6` detect the nonzero class `h5 n` in associated grade 6. Then:

- `a6^2` is in `F12 pi_124` by multiplicativity of the Adams filtration.
- `F12 pi_124 = F13 pi_124`, using the vanishing of `E4^(12,136)`.
- `F13/F14` is generated by the class detected by `e0 Delta h6 g`.
- Replacing `a6` by another representative changes it by `F7 = F8` in stem 62.
  Its square changes by `F16`. Therefore the coefficient of `a6^2` in `F13/F14`
  is independent of that replacement.
- The squares of the filtration-8 and filtration-10 directions lie in `F16`
  and `F20`, respectively. They cannot change the grade-13 coefficient.

The filtration-12 vanishing is stronger than merely observing `(h5 n)^2 = 0`
in Ext. Existing Lean source uses it in
`Main/Internal/Selected/ProductDetection.lean:97` via
`Computation.Standard.e4_stem124_af12`, together with the filtration-11
vanishing. `Main/Internal/Selected/ThetaFrames.lean:34` constructs the
grade-13 generator from the finite-page exhaustion argument. These source
proofs still inherit the registered table and model/comparison obligations.

Result: **pass**, relative to the cited homotopy, filtration, convergence,
and finite-page inputs. This provides a well-defined coefficient epsilon.
It does not determine epsilon and does not eliminate the choice-independent
grade-10 theta5-square obstruction or the grade-at-least-14 tail.

## 2. Theta5: Toda route and rejected shortcuts

comparison described in its Corollary 1.2. A statement about one page label
cannot silently become a statement about a specified homotopy representative.

The following conditional shuffle calculation is correct. Suppose actual
classes `n`, `theta4`, and `a` satisfy

    2 n = 0, 2 theta4 = 0, a in <n, 2, theta4>, pi_61 = 0.

Then `<2, theta4, theta4>` is zero, assuming `theta4^2=0` so this bracket is
defined. Toda shuffle gives `theta4 a = 0`. A second shuffle gives

Signs do not affect the final mod-two grade. The project has the complete

would prove the grade-13 square coefficient zero, even without identifying

## 3. Theta6: exact obstruction and negative implication

Let `G=pi_126(S)_(2)` and `H=F3 G`. Fix a theta6 detected by h6^2. All choices
are `theta6+H`, provided the known low-filtration quotient is exactly the
nonzero h6^2 direction. There is an order-two choice precisely when

    2 theta6 is in 2H.

This criterion is choice-independent: replacing theta6 by theta6+h changes
the obstruction by `2h`. It is an exact group-theoretic reformulation, not
a calculation of the obstruction.

For `C2=cofib(2:S->S)`, exactness gives

    pi_127(C2) --q--> pi_126(S) --2--> pi_126(S).

The negative implication is already rigorous without a geometric boundary
theorem, assuming the actual filtered comparison and low-filtration data:

1. An order-two theta6 has a lift `a` along q.
2. Since q preserves Adams filtration and theta6 has exact filtration 2,
   `a` is not in filtration 3.
3. In stem 127 of C2, associated grades 0 and 2 vanish and grade 1 has sole
   possible generator h7[0]. Thus a would be detected by h7[0].
4. Any certified nonzero differential out of h7[0] therefore rules out every
   order-two theta6 choice.

There are no incoming differentials to the filtration-1 h7[0] class for r>=2,
because their source filtration is `1-r<0`.

The positive implication from h7[0] permanence still needs the actual hidden
q-extension identifying its image with h6^2 in associated grade 2. The
sphere differential `d2(h7)=h0 h6^2` is the expected geometric-boundary input.
Ordinary E2 naturality has q(h7[0])=0 and cannot provide that identification.
For a hidden length-1 q-extension with target filtration 2, the source
filtration-2 vanishing excludes the only possible crossing (q does not lower
filtration). This checks the crossing arithmetic but does not itself supply
the filtered geometric-boundary theorem.

Result: **pass** for the exact obstruction and the conditional negative
implication; **unresolved comparison** for the positive implication.

differentials cannot be treated as zero. The top-cell projection of the

There is, however, a rigorous global stopping bound. Ravenel, Theorem 3.4.5(a)
(local `theta6/ravenel.txt:4834`) gives

    Ext_A^(s,t)(F2,F2)=0 when 0<t-s<2s-epsilon(s),
    epsilon=1 for s=0,1 mod 4; 2 for s=2 mod 4; 3 for s=3 mod 4.

The cohomology short exact sequence of C2 yields an Ext exact sequence whose
two neighbors of `Ext_A^(s,t)(H*C2,F2)` are sphere Ext at `(s,t)` and `(s,t-1)`.
For a C2 target in stem 126 these have stems 126 and 125. Both vanish for
`s>=64`, since `2s-epsilon(s)>=127` there. A differential from h7[0] has target
filtration `1+r`, so all r>=63 targets vanish. Therefore only d11 through d62
remain after the recorded survival through E11; the t<=200 data range is
large enough to contain every possible target (`t<=189`).

This bound does not promise that existing input determines the finitely many
remaining differentials, but it prevents an indefinite search through pages.

The worker initially listed only outgoing pages 11,16,17,24. That list was
withdrawn: a target whose earlier outgoing differential is unknown may
survive if that differential is zero, and cannot be excluded simply by
comparing the recorded first-unknown page with the proposed incoming page.
The native d11=0 branch subsequently advanced the source's first unknown
page to d13, exhibiting the undercount. Only the r<=62 mathematical cutoff
and a conservative E2/known-boundary target scan are accepted by this audit.
