1319: \begin{proof}
1320: \uses{sem:Literature:one-line-differentials,sem:Literature:two-line-classical-inputs,sem:Computation:bounded-comparison}
1321: \textit{The nonzero classical conclusion.} The independent classical input in
1322: \ref{sem:Literature:two-line-classical-inputs} gives a nonzero $d_3$ from
1323: the specified $h_0h_4$ to degree $(5,19)$. The bounded Ext archive gives
1324: one basis element there, namely $h_0d_0$, with $d_0$ the fixed archived
1325: generator in degree $(4,18)$. A one-dimensional $\mathbb F_2$ space has
1326: exactly one nonzero element, so the target is $h_0d_0$. In particular the
1327: zero conclusion is false. This proves that conclusion without assuming
1328: the extension relations whose use is being examined.
1329: 
1330: \textit{The extension representatives.} For the actual normalized
1331: map, the first finite relation comes from multiplication by $h_0$
1332: on the first lambda quotient. Apply the specified Bockstein
1333: formulas to the independent differentials
1334: $d_2(h_4)=h_0h_3^2$ and $d_3(h_0h_4)=h_0d_0$.
1335: Their naturality constructs full classes $a$ and $\beta$ with
1336: the prescribed labels and $[h_0]a=\lambda\beta$.
1337: The class $d_0$ has a full lift $D$: all its outgoing differentials
1338: vanish, by the bounded Ext calculation and the sphere vanishing line.
1339: The actual first-quotient product shows that $[h_0]D$ has label
1340: $h_0d_0$. Hence $a-\lambda D$ keeps the original source label,
1341: while its image is
1342: \[
1343: \lambda\bigl(\beta-[h_0]D\bigr)\in F^6\pi_{14,18}S^{0,0}.
1344: \]
1345: It therefore has zero target label in filtration five. This
1346: constructs the zero-target relation; it does not assert that this
1347: actual image is zero. The length-one extension from $d_0$ has
1348: nonzero target and is essential in its minimal-length range.
1349: 
1350: The article uses that shorter extension as a crossing and as the
1351: indeterminacy relating the two length-two targets. In an actual
1352: synthetic crossing assertion, both relations must be expressed
1353: at the same weight; essentiality after multiplying by lambda
1354: requires its own check. The final maximal-image paragraph
1355: additionally requires an actual zero-image lift. These assertions
1356: are separate from the construction of the two target-label
1357: relations, and the latter is not a proof of the former.
1358: 
1359: This argument concerns the specified zero-layer example. Transporting its relations to another quotient or weight does not by itself preserve essentiality or no-crossing, so it is not a claim of the same counterexample at every retained level.
1360: \end{proof}
