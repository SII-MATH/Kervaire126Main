Scope of the related interface family `finite-page-calculus`. This note does not assert equivalence with a broader paper statement or mark any proof complete.


- **mathematical_purpose**: Turn complete representative/basis information and actual differentials into finite-page vanishing, frames and nonvanishing.

- **interface_role**: Existing proved calculation library with specific fixed-source route consumers. This group is retained for those calculations, not claimed to be required by every new AdamsSynthetic declaration.

- **mathematical_strength_preserved**: pageHomologyIso identifies the actual next page with homology of the actual page differential.

- **mathematical_strength_preserved**: Kernel/image, completeness-of-representatives, basis and incoming-differential hypotheses remain explicit in the exact Lean statements.

- **mathematical_strength_preserved**: F₂ expansion and finite frame lemmas retain their coefficient and dimension assumptions; no arbitrary E₂ equivalence replaces those obligations.

- **boundary**: No direct current AdamsSynthetic use is claimed for each individual specialized frame theorem. The justification is the fixed-source computational consumer group and retained supporting proof calculus.

- **boundary**: This ledger does not claim the downstream calculation files have been migrated or compiled in this repository.
