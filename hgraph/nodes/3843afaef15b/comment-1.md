Source verification (2026-10-18): Source adaptation checked. The canonical nu-E Adams resolution can be tensored with C-tau^q. Its polynomial E1 terms truncate to exponents 0,...,q-1; the d1 calculation then gives the stated E2 strip. At weight exponent a=t-w, passing to page r inspects outgoing d_i for i<=r-1 whose target exponent is a+i-1. The condition a+r-2<q retains all these targets and all incoming sources, so the actual quotient projection is an isomorphism on that page. This is a deduction from the tower used in BHS Theorem A.8 and Corollary A.11, not a separately numbered safe-range theorem in that paper. Write the BHS coordinates as (s,k,omega) and the project coordinates as (s,t,w). The translation is k=t-s and omega=w-(t-s); tau is renamed lambda. Hence s-omega=t-w, and the BHS differential degree (r,-1,1) becomes (r,r-1,0). This verifies the mathematical source and its stated regrading, not the outstanding Lean comparison with the fixed project model. Existing Lean proof and transitive-dependency status must remain unchanged. Spectral-sequence page and E-infinity subquotient statements here do not assert convergence to untruncated homotopy groups; BHS Convention A.5 allows an arbitrary spectrum for Theorem A.8 and Corollaries A.9/A.11. Primary references: [BHS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.14116v3): Theorem A.8; Notation A.6; pages arXiv PDF pp. 71-72; published pp. 306-307; arXiv:1910.14116v3, 10 May 2022; checked against Acta Math. 231 (2023), 205-344 [BHS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.14116v3): Corollary A.11(1)-(3) and its proof; Notation A.10; pages arXiv PDF p. 73; published p. 308; arXiv:1910.14116v3, 10 May 2022; checked against Acta Math. 231 (2023), 205-344 [LWX126](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.10879v2#S3): Theorem 3.6 and Remark 3.7; pages local PDF p. 16; arXiv:2412.10879v2, 22 February 2025; local AimPaper/main.tex and PDF also checked
