This is the ordinary triple Massey product in homology of the page-(r-1) differential, with r >= 3; it does not claim the distinct r = 2 cobar construction. Moss p.284 gives exactly this distinction and the quotient by both product summands. The project degree i+j+k-(r-1,r-2) equals Moss (p,q+1), where p=sum(s)-r+1 and q=sum(t)-r+1. Primary references: [Moss](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN266833020_0115/LOG_0047.pdf): Section 1; Theorem (1.2) and conditions (1.3); pages 283–285; Original Math.
