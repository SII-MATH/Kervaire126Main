**Formal scope:** The finite binding defines the triangle data, including its two compatibility laws; the fixed choice is supplied by the already bound quotient system. The untruncated inclusions, boundaries and distinguishedness are the actual cofiber constructions. These bindings do not assert a limit comparison or complete the registered Standard.quotients construction.
