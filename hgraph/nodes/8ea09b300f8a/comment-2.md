Moss p.285 gives weak convergence for the mod-p cohomology Adams system when the source is finite and target cohomology is finitely generated degreewise; spheres satisfy those conditions. The project expresses this as residual injectivity on its chosen tower, matching Belmont--Kong Definition 2.3. Composition detection is derived here from the fixed mapping pairing and its abutment compatibility; it is not a separate conclusion of Moss Theorem 1.2.
