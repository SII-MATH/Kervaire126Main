**Formal scope:** The two public bindings separately prove the finite extension with its E3 cycle qualification, and the existence of an actual cofiber class with the displayed top-cell image, cofiber d2 and permanent bottom-cell target. Both use the same independently constructed full representatives. They do not identify an arbitrary preselected AH lift, assert a nonzero bottom-cell target, or reproduce the article's secondary-Steenrod-algebra calculation. This n=2 result is distinct from the existing n=3 Main theorem.
