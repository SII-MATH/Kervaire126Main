Scope of the related interface family `permanence-and-infinity-representatives`. This note does not assert equivalence with a broader paper statement or mark any proof complete.


- **mathematical_purpose**: Keep a named finite-page label tied to the same infinite-cycle representative and its actual E∞ image.

- **interface_role**: NonzeroSurvival and NextPageRelation are exact representative predicates; permanence and infinity uniqueness are proved operations.

- **mathematical_strength_preserved**: NonzeroSurvival requires a single z in Z∞ representing the named E₂ element whose E∞ image is nonzero. Independently chosen survivors on each page would be weaker and are not substituted.

- **mathematical_strength_preserved**: nonzeroSurvival_of_survivesTo keeps both incoming and outgoing tail-differential vanishing assumptions.

- **mathematical_strength_preserved**: infinity_projection_eq_of_page_projection_eq compares two Z∞ representatives through their equality on one finite quotient.

- **mathematical_strength_preserved**: NextPageRelation uses one successor-cycle representative for both quotient classes. It does not supply an unrestricted transition map on all page elements.

- **boundary**: No global proof of permanence or concrete Adams convergence is manufactured by the interface.
