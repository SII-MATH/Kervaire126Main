Source verification (2026-10-18): Partial source correspondence. Toda Corollary 3.7 gives the uniform classical order-two symmetric-bracket law. Baues--Muro Definition 1.2, relation (T6), and Theorem 1.3 supply the connective-ring-spectrum version with the image of the stable Hopf element. To apply it in the synthetic category, one uses the canonical stable enrichment (or the corresponding square-zero mapping module) and identifies that Hopf element in pi_(1,0) with lambda^2 eta using BHS Proposition A.20. AimPaper proof of Lemma 7.16 uses the displayed synthetic formula for theta-five. The bound Lean statement is broader: every theta in pi_(62,64) killed by two, without a theta-five detection hypothesis. The cited uniform law explains this extension, but the canonical-enrichment and selected-Hopf-class comparison is an additional source-to-project obligation, not a quoted assertion of Lemma 7.16.
