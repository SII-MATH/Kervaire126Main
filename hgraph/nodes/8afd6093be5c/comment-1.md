**Formal scope:** The three public nu theorems exclude the actual finite and infinite crossing witnesses in the stated positions; the multiplication-by-two theorem supplies the essential short crossing and both long target presentations. These conclusions do not assert actual synthetic no-crossing in another quotient, weight or level and do not use the original Proposition 5.10.
