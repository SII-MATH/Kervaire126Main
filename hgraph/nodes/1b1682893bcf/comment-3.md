Scope of the related interface family `obligation-af-detection`. This note does not assert equivalence with a broader paper statement or mark any proof complete.


- **mathematical_purpose**: The map f is represented in pi_0 of the actual internal hom F(X,Y) for B. Same-P mapping filtration comparison and finite-scope MappingSpectrumSystem convergence give a common Z-infinity representative with nonzero E-infinity image detecting the same map at (s,s). Exact detection is derived from the associated-graded quotient and convergence; finite exact filtration uses explicit separation.

- **constraints**: The map f is represented in pi_0 of the actual internal hom F(X,Y) for B. Same-P mapping filtration comparison and finite-scope MappingSpectrumSystem convergence give a common Z-infinity representative with nonzero E-infinity image detecting the same map at (s,s). Exact detection is derived from the associated-graded quotient and convergence; finite exact filtration uses explicit separation.

- **constraints**: Old nonzero-only detection was invalid for odd-torsion maps and referred to Y rather than F(X,Y). Require finite X,Y and non-odd-torsion, and produce an actual detection witness. detects_exact is a theorem, not an extra field.

- **roles**: explicit-input-or-derived-consumer-contract
