Scope of the related interface family `coefficient-tower-presentation`. This note does not assert equivalence with a broader paper statement or mark any proof complete.


- **mathematical_purpose**: 给定HF₂单位纤维塔，保留实际Z₂ ambient、全部cycles/boundaries与lift微分的比较。

- **constraints**: stageAt s = stage s.toNat; negative stages are constant, so HF2 layer isomorphisms apply only to natural stages.

- **constraints**: Layer is the chosen cofiber of that same transition and the layer iso identifies its inclusion with the coefficient-unit smash map.

- **constraints**: The spectral ambient is isomorphic to the actual Z2 submodule, and every finite Z/B membership is fixed by the raw tower.

- **constraints**: Differentials use the actual connecting lift and cofiber image, including zero representatives.

- **constraints**: Classical naturality compares the same family morphism to the selected stage/cofiber map; synthetic natural_e2 allows their raw representatives to differ by an actual B2 boundary, retaining the original quotient-level law.
