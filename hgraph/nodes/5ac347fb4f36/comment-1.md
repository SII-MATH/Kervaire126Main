Source verification (2026-10-18): Source adaptation checked. The fixed h-zero choice is normalized by the first-quotient label and lambda[h-zero]=2, as in AimPaper Example 5.8(4). BHS Proposition A.20 gives a complete low synthetic ring presentation, with relation (0) h-zero eta=0 and relation (9) tau h-zero=2.
