\chapter{Geometry --- Framed Manifolds of Kervaire Invariant One}
Browder's theorem relates the existence of framed manifolds of Kervaire invariant one to the survival of the classes $h_j^2$ in the Adams spectral sequence. In these statements from \cite{LWX126}, spectra are assumed to be 2-completed, connective, and of finite type. The three statements in this chapter have not yet been formalized.
