\chapter{E2Computation --- The E2 Page and Algebraic Inputs}
In statements quoted from \cite{LWX126}, spectra are assumed to be $2$-completed, connective and of finite type. Write $E_2^{s,t}$ for the mod-two Adams $E_2$-page, with stem $t-s$. This chapter records the specified Ext generators, bases, products and their comparisons with the sphere and cofiber pages. The bounded sphere comparison is defined for $t\leq261$. These algebraic inputs precede the proof tools; later Adams differentials and survival calculations appear in Chapter~6.

\section{Archived generators and bounded coordinates}


\begin{definition}[The fixed source archive and its complete coordinates]
\label{sem:Computation:fixed-archive}
\level{coarse}
From \cite{LinProgram}, archived sphere generators and bases.\par
Fix bigraded $\mathbb F_2$-modules $P^{s,t}$ with their specified bilinear
products, unit and named atoms. The finite generator catalogue records the
index, name and Adams bidegree of each generator; the selected atoms
$h_i,g,e_0,\Delta h_1g,\Delta h_6g,Md_0,[B_4],b,x_{n,s,j}$ have their
prescribed catalogue records. A source monomial is formed from the unit,
generators and degree-preserving products, and is evaluated using these
same source operations and generator values.

The fixed sphere archive supplies the complete catalogue, the atom indices,
the basis counts and every basis monomial in each bidegree. The generator
selected for an atom has exactly its prescribed name, degree and source
value. For $s,t\in\mathbb N$ with $t\leq261$, its source coordinates give
\[
P^{s,t}\cong\bigoplus_{i<\operatorname{basisCount}(s,t)}\mathbb F_2.
\]
The inverse image of each coordinate unit vector is the value of the
corresponding archived monomial. Thus these monomials form a complete
basis of this specified source component.
