\chapter{AdamsSynthetic --- The Adams and Synthetic Framework}
We work with the classical stable category $\mathrm{Sp}$, the synthetic category $\mathrm{Syn}$, and their mod-two Adams towers. Fix a map $\nu_3\colon S^3\to S$ and a pointed detecting spectrum $D$. Let $\mathcal C_0$ consist of the sphere, the cofiber of $\nu_3$, $D$, and their integer suspensions. Let $\mathcal S_0$ be generated by the synthetic sphere and $\nu(\mathcal C_0)$ under bidegree shifts and finite $\lambda$ quotients. Convergence assertions below are restricted to these object families unless further hypotheses are stated.

In the statements quoted from \cite{LWX126}, spectra are assumed to be $2$-completed, connective, and of finite type. Original numbering and context are available in \href{paper/main.tex.html}{the source text} and \href{paper/2412.10879.pdf}{the paper}.

Consider $f: X\to Y$, $x\in E_\infty^{s,t}(X)$, $y\in E_\infty^{s+n,t+n}(Y)$ and $y'\in E_\infty^{s+m,t+m}(Y)$ for $m,n\ge 0$.

\begin{notation}[Quotient and boundary maps]
\label{notation:AdamsSynthetic:quotient-boundary-maps}
\label{not:deltaandrho}
\level{coarse}
From \cite{LWX126}, Notation 4.1.\par
There are certain special maps between synthetic spectra that we want to consider. For any $n<m\le \infty$ and a spectrum $X$, we have the following  distinguished triangles of $\HF$-synthetic spectra
$$\Sus^{0,-n}\nu X/\lambda^{m-n}\fto{\lambda^n} \nu X/\lambda^m\fto{~\rho_{n,m}~} \nu X/\lambda^n\fto{~\delta_{n,m}~} \Sus^{1,-n}\nu X/\lambda^{m-n}.$$
We simply write $\rho=\rho_{n,m}$, $\delta=\delta_{n,m}$ by abuse of notation if $n,m$ is understood in the context.

$$\Sus^{0,-n}\nu X\fto{\lambda^n} \nu X\fto{~\rho~} \nu X/\lambda^n\fto{~\delta~} \Sus^{1,-n}\nu X.$$

Many arguments for finite $m$ implies the corresponding statement when $m=\infty$ because of
$$\nu X=\varprojlim_{m} \nu X/\lambda^m$$
as shown in \cite[Proposition A.13]{BHS}.

We also note that our grading for the triangulation translation functor is smashing with $S^{1,0}$, which is consistent with \cite[Appendix A]{BHS} but is different from \cite[Section 7]{BurklundXu}.
