  16 # Bounded extension spectral sequences
  17 
  18 This is the axiom-free migration of the usable declarations from
  19 `KIPBase/SpectralSequence/BoundedExtension.lean`.  The historical names are
  20 preserved in `KIP126.Core.SpectralSequence`, while the implementation reuses
  21 KIP126's canonical filtered-complex machinery.
  22 
  23 The old declarations `BoundedExtensionSS.essDiff` and
  24 `BoundedExtensionSS.essBoundary` had unfinished object-valued bodies, so
  25 they do not yet have a mathematical definition to migrate.  Consequently the
  26 dependent `HasFExtension`, commutativity API, and the old functors whose map
  27 proofs were also placeholders are deliberately outside this migration
  28 frontier.  No replacement zero object or project-defined axiom is introduced.
  29 -/
